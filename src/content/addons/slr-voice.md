detail:
  - heading: "How to use it"
    body: "Join a world with voice chat connected and run `/slrvoice enroll`. The System asks you to say each phrase a few times — hold the **Voice Command** key (default `'`), speak, release.\n\nIn play it is the same motion: hold the key, say *\"Arise\"*, release. The shadows rise immediately and in silence."
  - heading: "Nobody else hears you"
    body: "Simple Voice Chat's proximity transmission is cancelled for exactly as long as the key is held, so the phrase stays between you and the System. Other players hear nothing.\n\nThe interception only happens while the key is held, so ordinary proximity chat is untouched and other voice addons following the same rule can share the microphone."
  - heading: "It learns your voice, not English"
    body: "Recognition is acoustic rather than linguistic — the mod has no dictionary, so it can never mishear one word as another. It only knows what you taught it.\n\nThat is why the phrase list matters: add every way you actually say it out loud. Any entry firing casts Arise.\n\nThresholds are not guessed either. Enrollment measures how much your own repeats differ from each other and derives the accept threshold from that, so it self-tunes to your voice, your microphone and your room."
  - heading: "A spoken cast is not a keybind press"
    body: "The bridge tells SoloCraft the player performed the incantation themselves. Arise then skips its own recorded \"Arise\" cue and the twelve-tick delay that syncs to it, so the shadows rise on **your** word rather than a playback. It also bypasses the sneak-to-scan modifier, which has no spoken equivalent.\n\nMechanics are untouched: class, mana, shadow storage and cooldown behave exactly as they do from the keybind."
  - heading: "Cheating gains nothing"
    body: "The client only ever *requests* a cast. The server allow-lists the skill, throttles the sender, and hands off to SoloCraft, which applies every real check. A modified client gains nothing it could not get by pressing the keybind."
